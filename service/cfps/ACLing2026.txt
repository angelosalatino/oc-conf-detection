SCOPE
------------------------------------------------------------------
We invite original research on computational linguistics, natural
language processing (NLP), and the development, evaluation, and
application of language models.
NEW THIS YEAR: Applications of pretrained language models, LLMs, and
vision-language models to problems in other fields are welcome. Examples
include healthcare, education, cybersecurity, business, engineering,
agriculture, and environmental sustainability. Application papers do not
need to propose a new model. However, the language model must play a
central methodological role, and the paper must make a clear scientific
contribution supported by appropriate evaluation.
Work on any language is welcome, including Arabic, multilingual,
cross-lingual, code-switching, and low-resource settings. Papers and
presentations must be in English.
------------------------------------------------------------------
TOPICS (including but not limited to)
------------------------------------------------------------------
Language Models and Generative AI
Pretrained models and LLMs (architecture, optimization, applications);
fine-tuning, domain adaptation, and efficient models; prompt
engineering and reinforcement learning; RAG and knowledge grounding;
agents, tool use, and multi-agent systems
Applications Across Disciplines
Language models for decision support and scientific discovery;
domain-specific NLP; AI-augmented writing, reading, and translation tools
Core NLP
Syntax, parsing, semantics, pragmatics, and commonsense reasoning;
statistical and neural language modeling; question answering and
knowledge extraction; summarization and generation; text
classification, topic modeling, and document understanding; text
mining and information retrieval
Multilingual, Speech, and Multimodal
Machine translation and cross-lingual NLP; multilinguality,
code-switching, and low-resource languages; speech, dialogue, and
conversational AI; vision-language models and multimodal AI
Social and Affective Computing
Sentiment analysis and emotion recognition; social media NLP and
computational social science
Trustworthy and Responsible AI
Robustness, bias, and hallucination; interpretability and
explainability; benchmarking and evaluation; ethical and
human-centered AI
Accepted paper types: theory, algorithms, architectures, applications,
resources, datasets, benchmarks, evaluation studies, rigorous surveys,
and position papers.

SUBMISSION GUIDELINES
------------------------------------------------------------------

    Original, unpublished work not under review elsewhere
    Full papers only (abstract-only submissions are not accepted)
    Official ACLing 2026 single-column template (Word or LaTeX):
        https://acling.org/submission/
    Length: 3–8 pages, including figures, tables, and references.
        Up to 2 extra pages (10 maximum) are allowed for an additional fee.
    Double-blind review: manuscripts must be anonymized
    At least one author must register and present the paper for it to
        appear in the proceedings
